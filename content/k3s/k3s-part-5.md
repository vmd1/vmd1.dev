As part of my planned uphaul of VEX TM Tools, I need fast, and large amounts of storage. Object storage is the best solution to this, being both standardized and scalable. Now, obviously, I could just use Cloudflare R2 or AWS S3, but where's the fun in that? This also provides me with a location for me to take backups.
